# Using the manufacturer manuals - copyright rules (all agents)

The manuals in this folder are the manufacturers' copyright. They are here for
reference only. The app and the repository must never contain them.

1. **Never copy the PDFs, page renders or extracted text into the repository**,
   and never into `public/` or anything that ships or deploys. Read them only
   from this scratchpad folder.
2. **Facts, in our own words.** Specs, ranges, units, panel labels, jack names,
   normals, signal flow and parameter lists are facts. Use them freely, written
   in your own words, tables and code.
3. **Quotes: small and rare.** At most one short sentence (25 words or fewer) at
   a time, only where the exact wording matters, always cited as
   "(<document>, p.N)". Never reproduce paragraphs, tables, lists of steps or
   whole sections.
4. **No copied artwork.** Don't trace or embed diagrams, panel drawings, patch
   sheets, photos or logos. Draw panels and diagrams from the facts, in our own
   style.
5. **Presets and patches:** recreate the *settings* (facts). Describe them in
   your own words. Use descriptive names, mentioning the source only in the
   description, for example "Based on the manual's 'Quick Hats' patch (p.33)".
   Rename anything that looks like a branded or distinctive title.
6. **Lessons and help text are original writing.** The manuals can inform the
   facts, never the prose.
7. In REFERENCE.md, cite the document and page for each fact you verified
   ("verified: A4 MKII manual p.62").
